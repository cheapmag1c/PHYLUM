# PHYLUM 1.6 — SOCIUS

**Knowledge endured. Now relationships can outlive the individual.**

SOCIUS adds persistent social organization without scripting society or civilization. A lineage may remain solitary forever. Stable groups only become possible when population size, NERVE cognition, social recognition/cooperation, SOMA life history and TECHNE cultural persistence make them viable.

## New simulation layer

- Persistent social groups that can survive turnover of individual organisms.
- Group ancestry, fission and social collapse distinct from biological speciation/extinction.
- Social territories nested inside biological species ranges.
- Group identity, cohesion, tolerance, coordination and emergent coordination styles.
- Bounded cultural norms grounded in behaviors and TECHNE practices already present.
- Persistent group-to-group relationships, including tolerance, cooperation, competition and antagonism.
- Weak demographic feedback so social organization can matter without overpowering ecology, climate or disease.
- A species can survive genetically while a social lineage disappears.

## ORRERY graphical overhaul

SOCIUS ships with **ORRERY**, a complete visual revision of the generated Observatory:

- New 1800×1160 composite world atlas.
- Relief/biome cartography and cleaner plate/contour presentation.
- Species range cores and fossil ghost ranges.
- SOCIUS group territories and social relationship arcs.
- TECHNE sites and ruins as material-history markers.
- Disease and WITNESS event overlays.
- Rebuilt deep-time phylogeny with SOMA/NERVE/TECHNE/SOCIUS markers.
- Dedicated SOCIUS social-lineage plate and browser.
- Rebuilt GitHub Pages Observatory with layer controls and system navigation.

SOCIUS is not a government system, a war game, or a civilization tech tree. No political form, settlement, institution or technology is unlocked at a fixed generation.
