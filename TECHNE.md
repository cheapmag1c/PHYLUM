# PHYLUM 1.5 — TECHNE

**Life learned. Now knowledge can outlive the organism.**

TECHNE is PHYLUM's cultural-inheritance and material-trace layer. It sits above NERVE rather than replacing it.

- **PALEON / DEEP TIME 2.0** — planet, climate, geology and ecological context
- **SOMA** — bodies, physiology, development and life history
- **NERVE** — perception, memory, learning and social behavior
- **TECHNE** — persistent learned knowledge, cultural ancestry, construction and archaeology
- **WITNESS** — observation and history

## Core rules

TECHNE is **not a technology tree**. Nothing unlocks at a fixed generation. A cultural practice can only appear when the living lineage already has compatible NERVE cognition, SOMA anatomy, social transmission and environmental opportunity.

Cultural information is distinct from genetic information. Practices can:

- survive a biological speciation event through founder transmission;
- spread between contacting biological lineages;
- diverge into a separate cultural lineage without speciation;
- disappear during demographic bottlenecks, epidemics or weak transmission;
- alter later natural selection through weak gene-culture feedback.

## Cultural practices

Potential practices include persistent nesting, food caching, route marking, alarm dialects, object selection, object modification, constructed shelter, resource tending, water control and rarer advanced material practices.

The most advanced possibilities are strongly gated. Controlled combustion, compound tools or symbolic marking are **possible**, not promised. A lineage can remain culturally simple forever.

## Archaeology

Material practices can generate durable aggregate sites. TECHNE tracks sites as active structures and then ruins. Sites can outlive the lineage or practice that created them, giving PHYLUM an archaeological record alongside its fossil record.

The simulation stores bounded site aggregates rather than one object per organism, preserving deep-time performance.

## Observation

TECHNE generates:

- `renders/techne.svg` — current cultural record;
- `docs/techne.html` — searchable cultural/archaeological browser;
- `docs/techne-data.json` — static data source;
- `python -m phylum techne` — CLI cultural state.
